# Key Takeaways: “FCAJ x Agentic AI Build Week: Show Up. Build. Pitch. WIN!”

### Event Purpose

* **Building Real-World Agentic AI Products:** A 24-hour Hackathon organized by the FCAJ community in collaboration with AWS and JI Fund. The event provided developers and business minds with a hands-on experience to form teams, brainstorm ideas, and build functional Agentic AI solutions addressing real-world problems.
* **Connecting the Builder Community:** Created a networking environment for AI engineers, Fullstack developers, security experts, and business-minded individuals across various universities and enterprises.
* **Fostering Innovation & Creativity:** Encouraged young talents to challenge traditional operational models and leverage modern AI tools and cloud architectures on AWS.


### Speakers & Presenting Teams

**Special Speakers & Guests:**
* **Mr. Joseph Marazzotta:** Head of Technology, AWS ASEAN.
* **Mr. Nguyen Gia Hung:** Head of Solution Architecture, AWS Vietnam.

**Outstanding Teams Sharing Solutions & Experiences:**
* **One Team (1st Place AWS Track):** Shared their omni-channel conversational ordering chatbot solution for KFC.
* **Signal Scout (2nd Place AWS Track):** Team from FPT University presenting a Multi-Agent system for competitive market analysis.
* **Plan B:** Showcased an AI app assisting Solution Architects by automatically designing system architecture and generating Terraform code.
* **3K / Shepherd:** Student group presenting a crowd monitoring and flow management system via cameras using Computer Vision & AI Agents.
* **Six Pillars:** Demonstrated an Adaptive Workflow Engine supporting Anti-Money Laundering (AML) investigations for banking.


### Key Highlights

#### A. Inspirational Message from AWS ASEAN (Mr. Joseph Marazzotta)
* **Shift in Development Paradigm:** Unlike 20 years ago when banking systems released software quarterly, the Agentic AI era enables process automation and continuous releases by the minute.
* **Challenging Traditional Barriers:** Young builders should shed legacy mindsets and bring fresh innovation to traditional sectors like retail, banking & finance, and FMCG.
* **Human in the Loop:** Citing Amazon's fleet of over 1 million robots in fulfillment centers: robots and AI are merely tools; humans remain essential for providing strategic direction and decisions.
* **Lifelong Learning Mindset:** Participating in a Hackathon is just the beginning; the key is maintaining a daily learning habit to lead change.

#### B. Featured Agentic AI Solutions
* **One Team (KFC Conversational Agent):** Solved user pain points caused by app switching during food ordering. The system enables natural ordering via Zalo/WhatsApp, integrates Agent Core for order history, uses TinyFish for menu scraping, and reduced infrastructure costs by 75% using AWS Serverless.
* **Signal Scout (Competitive Intelligence Canvas):** Used Multi-Agents to gather fragmented competitor signals across the internet, automatically generating market analysis canvases, ROI forecasts, and strategic risk assessments.
* **Plan B (SA Professional AI App):** Assisted Solution Architects with urgent client requests by analyzing natural language documents, auto-generating architecture diagrams on Draw.io, calculating costs, and producing IaC/Terraform code for automated deployment.
* **3K (Shepherd - Crowd Flow Management):** Combined AWS Kinesis Video Streams, Fargate running YOLO/ByteTrack, and Amazon Bedrock Agent to monitor crowd density at airports/supermarkets and trigger real-time staff dispatch alerts.
* **Six Pillars (AML Adaptive Workflow Engine):** Addressed the 90-95% false positive rate in anti-money laundering investigations. A Multi-Agent workflow coordinated with AWS Bedrock Guardrails, Step Functions, and OpenSearch to automate KYC checks, transaction tracing, sanctions list screening, and proof report generation.


### Key Learnings

* **Business-First Approach:** No matter how complex technology gets, it cannot succeed without solving core business needs. Products must focus on real pain points rather than technology for its own sake or academic theory.
* **Scope Management & MVP Strategy:** Within a 24-hour limit, controlling project scope is vital. Prioritize delivering a stable Minimum Viable Product (MVP/POC) for live demos rather than over-scoping and leaving things unfinished.
* **Teamwork & Collaboration:** Learn to put ego aside, listen to teammates, and divide roles clearly (AI Engineer, Fullstack, Security, Pitching) to reach a shared goal.
* **Cost Optimization & Cloud Native Architecture:** Leveraging Cloud Native services (AWS Bedrock, Agent Core, Lambda, DynamoDB) provides high scalability, strong security, and better operational cost-efficiency compared to relying heavily on third-party tools.
* **Can-Do Spirit:** Whether participating for the first time, winning an award, or not placing, every team demonstrated a bold "can-do" spirit to build ideas together—an inspiring mindset worth learning from.


### Event Experience

* **Direct Learning from Experts:** Gained best practices directly from AWS ASEAN and AWS Vietnam leaders on modern app design, Multi-Agent architecture, and Cloud cost optimization.
* **High-Energy Hackathon Atmosphere:** Experienced 24 hours of non-stop work under time pressure: from brainstorming, overnight coding, fixing Git conflicts, drinking Red Bull, eating KFC, to polishing pitch slides right before the deadline.
* **Intense Pitching & Q&A Sessions:** Faced deep-dive questions from local and international judges on system architecture, UX design, business viability, and AI hallucination mitigation.
* **Networking & Connections:** An incredible environment to exchange experiences with mentors and peers, build lasting relationships, and develop a common language between tech and business roles.
