### Event Purpose

To bring a fresh and engaging experience while upholding the spirit of learning and sharing for interns through team-based competitive trivia.


### Key Highlights

#### Interactive Game Session on Cloud Architect Concepts

Two teams competed head-to-head, answering sets of questions ranging from basic to advanced levels. The team with the higher score advanced to the next round.


### Event Experience

- The questions throughout the game rounds provided valuable new knowledge for newcomers exploring Cloud concepts and AWS services.
- The engaging quiz format, featuring progressively challenging questions, offered practical insights that can be applied to future projects.


#### Networking and Discussion
- The session provided a valuable opportunity to interact directly with industry experts, peers, and business team members, helping to **build a ubiquitous language** between business and technical roles.
- Through real-world examples, I gained a clearer appreciation of the **business-first approach**—always starting with core business needs rather than focusing solely on technology.
